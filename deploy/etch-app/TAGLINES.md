# Etch tagline candidates (not deployed, hero unchanged)

Direction (Pranav, 10-16): engineers ideate out loud, and every decision they make is an actual PCB. Like Wispr Flow: talk, let go, it's done.

1. **Say it. It's a board.**
   Every idea you speak comes back as a real PCB that already passed its checks.

2. **Every decision is a real board.**
   Change your mind as often as you like. Each version is routed, checked and ready to fab.

3. **Think out loud in PCBs.**
   Talk through the idea, and Etch keeps every version as a board you can step back to.
